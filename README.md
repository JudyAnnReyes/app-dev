**SpongeBob SquarePants** is my favorite animated series. It is a *comedy and adventure cartoon* about a cheerful sea sponge who lives in a pineapple under the sea in Bikini Bottom and works as a fry cook at the Krusty Krab.

- Positive themes of friendship and optimism
